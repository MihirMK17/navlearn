# Venues and Deadlines

## Primary Target: IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L)

- **Submission**: Rolling (no fixed deadline — submit when ready)
- **Page limit**: 6 pages + references (letter format)
- **Review time**: ~3-4 months
- **Impact factor**: ~5.2 (2024)
- **URL**: https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ra-l
- **Notes**: High visibility in robotics community. Strong fit for system/benchmark papers.
  Accepted papers often presented at ICRA or IROS.

## Backup: IROS 2027

- **Venue**: TBD (typically October)
- **Submission deadline**: ~March 2027
- **Page limit**: 6 pages + references
- **Notes**: Good backup if RA-L review takes too long or is rejected.
  Start RA-L submission first; pivot to IROS if needed.

## arXiv Preprint (Parallel)

- **Submit**: immediately once draft is complete (~Paper 1 Introduction done)
- **Category**: cs.RO (Robotics)
- **Notes**: Counts as prior art. Citable immediately. Good for O-1A evidence.
  arXiv submission does NOT preclude RA-L submission.
